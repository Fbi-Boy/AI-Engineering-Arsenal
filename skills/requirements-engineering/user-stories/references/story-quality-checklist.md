# User Story Quality Checklist

## User and value
- Is the user / stakeholder role real and relevant?
- Is the goal something the role needs to achieve?
- Does the benefit explain why the capability matters?
- Does the story distinguish current pain from proposed solution?

## Scope and refinement
- Does the story cover one coherent outcome?
- Is it small enough for the team's planning horizon?
- Are meaningful dependencies visible?
- Are important rules, states, and exceptions linked?
- Does it avoid premature implementation detail?
- Is a spike required for unresolved technical or product uncertainty?

## Testability
- Are acceptance criteria present or clearly identified as missing?
- Can the key outcome be demonstrated?
- Are permission, validation, empty, and failure paths considered when relevant?
- Are vague quality expectations replaced with measures or open questions?

## INVEST review
- Independent enough to plan?
- Negotiable solution details?
- Valuable outcome?
- Estimable with current evidence?
- Small enough?
- Testable?

Record the reason where a dimension is currently weak. Do not reject an otherwise valuable story solely because it has a real dependency.

## Backlog hygiene
- Stable ID and useful title.
- Parent epic / requirement traceability.
- Priority and status are distinct.
- No invented estimate, owner, sprint, or deadline.
- Technical and quality enablers remain visible.
- Duplicate and conflicting stories are identified.
- Approval state is evidence-based.
